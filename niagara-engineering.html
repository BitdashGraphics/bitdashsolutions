[ JACE / SUPERVISOR / INTEGRATION ]

Structure first.
Then control.

A Niagara station needs a consistent relationship between equipment, points, schedules, alarms, histories and operator graphics. We begin with the approved design and point schedule so naming and behavior follow the project rather than growing by accident.

At the JACE level, scope can include station organization, supported device integration and control logic where appropriate to the architecture. At the Supervisor level, we coordinate the agreed site structure, shared navigation, alarm presentation and historical information. Controller-native programming is treated as a separate, explicit scope.

For existing systems, we review station backups and the installed Niagara release before proposing changes. Driver availability, license capacity, module compatibility and device documentation must be confirmed. An upgrade or migration is a planned change with backup, validation and rollback requirements.

[ SCOPE & DELIVERABLES ]

From database.
To daily operation.

01

Station & equipment structure

Define station folders, equipment relationships, point naming, units and reusable conventions around owner standards and the approved equipment list.

02

Point integration & mapping

Develop the mapping for supported BACnet, Modbus or other licensed interfaces. Record device identifiers, object/register references, scaling, units and read/write intent.

03

Sequence implementation

Translate approved sequences into the agreed Niagara logic scope, including operating modes, setpoints, time delays, interlocks and alarm conditions. Identify controller-level dependencies before implementation.

04

Alarms, schedules & histories

Configure the agreed schedules, alarm priorities, routing requirements and history intervals. Confirm retention, storage capacity and operator expectations with the owner.

05

Operator graphics & navigation

Connect equipment graphics, system overviews and navigation to the engineered point structure. Define command permissions and operator context rather than exposing uncontrolled writable points.

06

Remote checks & handover

Support point validation and approved functional tests with your local team. Organize station backups, revision notes, issue logs and agreed operator handover information.

[ PROJECT INPUTS ]

A productive
first review.

  1. Installed Niagara version, JACE/Supervisor details and current station backups.
  2. Approved sequences, mechanical drawings, equipment list and point/I/O schedule.
  3. Controller information, integration manuals and available driver/module licenses.
  4. Owner naming, graphics, alarm, schedule and history standards.
  5. IT-approved remote access, permission boundaries and an on-site testing contact.

Do not send passwords or access credentials through the inquiry form. Access arrangements are coordinated separately through approved channels.

[ VALIDATION ]

Check the behavior.
Keep the evidence.

Review before download

Check mappings, units, logic paths and graphics bindings against the agreed baseline. Record assumptions that depend on field conditions rather than treating them as verified.

Test with the local team

Coordinate safe point checks and approved mode transitions. Compare commands, proofs, temperatures and trend evidence. Physical overrides and equipment operation remain with authorized local personnel.

Agree acceptance

Track defects and retests against the approved sequence. Close out with the agreed backups, documentation and open-item list. Remote commissioning support does not replace physical inspection or statutory acceptance responsibilities.
