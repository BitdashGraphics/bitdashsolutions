Ask us anything ↗
Can you support only part of our project?

Yes. Engage us for a defined deliverable—drawings, sequences, programming, HMI graphics, or remote commissioning support—or bring those disciplines together in one coordinated scope.

What do you need to get started?

Available drawings, specifications, equipment and point schedules, platform details, project milestones, and your current challenges. The project brief below helps organize those inputs; we clarify gaps during scoping.

How does remote commissioning work?

We coordinate with your on-site personnel to review software, guide functional checks, inspect trends, and track issues. Remote access arrangements, test windows, and responsibility for physical equipment are agreed before work begins.

Which building automation platforms do you support?

We support Tridium Niagara 4, Alerton, Honeywell WEBs, Honeywell Optimizer, Honeywell EBI, Honeywell CPO, TREND, Saia Burgess Controls, CentraLine, Honeywell EXCEL 5000, Ignition. We confirm the installed release, controllers, engineering tools, licenses, and interfaces when defining the project scope.

Are signed or sealed drawings included?

Any professional sign-off, jurisdiction-specific design responsibility, or sealed deliverable must be explicitly agreed in the project scope. Tell us your location and requirements so these can be reviewed before engagement.

How are pricing and timelines determined?

We scope each engagement around equipment, point count, integrations, deliverables, review cycles, and schedule. Send a project brief and we can discuss a proposal tailored to that scope.
