01 / DESIGN / DOCUMENT / COORDINATE

Controls engineering

Build a coordinated controls package around the mechanical design, equipment requirements, and the way the building will operate. We develop the documents your programmers, installers, and commissioning team can work from.

  • Controls drawings
  • Panel & wiring details
  • Point & I/O schedules

Sequences of operation

Turn design intent into an implementable controls narrative. Define what equipment should do, when it should do it, and how it should respond when conditions change.

  • Operating modes
  • Equipment control
  • Reset strategies

Programming & integration

Develop and organize building automation applications around approved sequences. Bring equipment, controller networks, and supervisory information into a consistent operator environment.

  • Niagara station development
  • DDC application programming
  • BACnet & Modbus integration

Analytics & optimization

Turn building trends into a useful engineering conversation. We structure data, develop agreed analytical checks, and help your team prioritize operating issues for verification.

  • Data readiness
  • Fault-detection rules
  • Niagara analytics configuration

Remote commissioning

Give your field team a focused engineering partner during checkout and functional testing. We support the software, evidence, and issue-resolution work remotely while on-site personnel perform physical verification.

  • Readiness review
  • Point-to-point support
  • Functional test scripts
